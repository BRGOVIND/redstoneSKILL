# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome people of different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code rather than people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of
private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and participation that
violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers without including unrelated personal information. Maintainers should
respond proportionately and protect the privacy of everyone involved.
